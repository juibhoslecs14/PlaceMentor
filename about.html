Who We Are

PlaceMentor is an AI-powered placement preparation platform specially designed for students who want to improve their interview skills, aptitude knowledge, communication and confidence before campus placements.

Our goal is to make placement preparation simple, interactive and stress-free. Instead of searching multiple websites, students can practice everything in one place with personalized learning and AI guidance.
